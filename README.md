## 👨🏾‍💻 About Me

I'm a **self-taught frontend and full-stack developer** from Nigeria, focused on building practical, revenue-ready web products — from SaaS platforms to e-commerce experiences. I work across the full stack with React, Supabase, Firebase, and payment integrations, and I document the journey on my YouTube channel for self-taught devs and aspiring founders.

- 🔭 Currently building **CoinPulse** — a real-time meme coin & crypto tracker SaaS with tiered subscriptions
- 🛒 Shipped **Masterpiece Shawarma** — a full e-commerce experience for a Lagos food brand (Paystack + Firebase)
- 🧠 Building **CogniTalk** — an AI-powered conversation app (Vite + React)
- 📺 Creator at **[@zerotowebhero_01](https://www.youtube.com/c/@zerotowebhero_01)** — practical dev content for self-taught builders
- 🌍 Based in **Nigeria** | Open to **remote roles & freelance contracts**
